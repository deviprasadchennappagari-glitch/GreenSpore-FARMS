Our mushrooms

Two varieties. One careful growing standard.

The first proposed crop mix pairs Oyster and Milky mushrooms—two varieties suited to a warm-climate pilot, each with its own growing needs.

01 · Pleurotus

Oyster Mushroom

Versatile and well suited to a straw-based growing cycle, Oyster mushrooms are planned as the larger share of the first crop. The project will select a warm-climate-tolerant strain with guidance from the spawn supplier or local experts.

First-cycle planning target
60 kg across all flushes
Planning yield
500 g per kg dry straw
Indicative bag count
120 bags at about 1 kg dry straw per bag
Growing focus
Fresh-air exchange, humidity, diffused light, and no direct sunlight

02 · Calocybe indica

Milky Mushroom

Milky mushrooms suit warm conditions and add a second crop to the planned mix. They require a casing stage after the substrate is fully colonised, using a clean and validated casing method.

First-cycle planning target
40 kg across all flushes
Planning yield
600 g per kg dry straw
Indicative bag count
67 bags at about 1 kg dry straw per bag
Growing focus
Warm fruiting conditions, humidity, ventilation, and hygienic casing

Yields, bag counts, and environmental conditions are planning assumptions, not guaranteed results or universal cultivation instructions. Actual performance depends on strain, substrate preparation, hygiene, room conditions, and cultivation protocol. Confirm strain-specific guidance with the spawn supplier or a local KVK before production.

From straw to harvest

A process designed around care.

  1. 01

    Prepare the substrate

    Use clean, dry, mature paddy straw and follow a validated preparation and pasteurisation protocol.

  2. 02

    Inoculate and incubate

    Use clean handling practices and keep incubation separate from fruiting environments.

  3. 03

    Manage crop-specific needs

    Monitor temperature, humidity, and fresh air at crop height. Apply casing to Milky bags after full spawn run.

  4. 04

    Harvest and record

    Weigh, grade, pack, and dispatch promptly. Track every batch and flush to compare actual results with the plan.
