Suggested next steps

Use evidence at every decision gate.

  1. Before construction: visit a KVK or ICAR-linked training centre, confirm strain and growing protocol, measure hot-season room conditions, obtain multiple quotes, and check current subsidy requirements with the District Horticulture Office.
  2. Pilot: build essential rooms and begin with 30–60 bags. Log contamination, flush yields, power, labour, buyer prices, and delivery results.
  3. Small commercial phase: expand only after two or three successful cycles and repeat buyers demonstrate demand and viable unit economics.
  4. Automation: add controls or larger equipment only when recorded operating data supports the investment.

Before building or selling, get local civil and electrical professional checks, confirm applicable approvals and current scheme eligibility, and validate food-business registration and labelling obligations.
